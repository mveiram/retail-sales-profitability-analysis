# Business Questions

## Core Business Question

What factors are driving sales and profitability, and where should the company focus its efforts?

## Analytical Questions

1. How do sales and profit evolve over time?
2. Which categories and sub-categories generate the most sales and profit?
3. Which products generate high sales but low or negative profit?
4. What is the relationship between discount levels and profitability?
5. Which regions and states contribute most to sales and profit?
6. Which customer segments generate the highest value?
7. Which customers contribute the most revenue and profit?
8. Where are the main opportunities and risks from a profitability perspective?
